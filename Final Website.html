Our Manufacturing & Quality Process

1. Processing & Blending

Raw materials undergo meticulous mixing, blending, and advanced particle size reduction to ensure a perfectly smooth finish.

2. Strict Quality Control

We execute rigorous testing at three distinct phases: incoming raw materials, in-process testing, and finished product analysis.

3. Testing & Documentation

Every single batch is supplied with comprehensive Test Reports and a formal Certificate of Analysis (COA) for total assurance.

4. Counter Samples

We retain physical counter samples of every manufactured batch for continuous, periodic evaluation to handle market feedback immediately.
